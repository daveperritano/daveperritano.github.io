The Lede

From the anchor desk to the boardroom

David spent a decade in local TV newsrooms across New York and North Carolina, producing live newscasts on hard deadlines and directing crews through breaking news and technical failures alike. That training — write tight, verify everything, never miss air — became the operating system for everything since.

He has since built a state agency's press operation from nothing to roughly 40 announcements a year, reintroduced a 140-year-old school's brand to a new generation of families, and is now bringing that same discipline to professional baseball as Communications & Marketing Manager of the Tri-City ValleyCats. Along the way: governor's-office releases, a mascot with a merch line, a student-run broadcast studio, and a magazine relaunched from a year-end report into a stewardship piece donors actually read.
